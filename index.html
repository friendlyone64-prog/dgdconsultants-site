How I work

The objective comes before the original idea.

A project often starts with an idea: acquire a competitor, enter a new market, find a partner or develop a new business.

The first question is not how to execute that idea. It is whether that is the best way to achieve the underlying business objective.

I work with the entrepreneur and management team to assess the original idea, understand the relevant variables and explore realistic alternatives.

If the idea stands up to the analysis, we develop it. If it does not, we look for another route. Sometimes the right conclusion is not to proceed.

Once the direction is clear, the decision is transformed into a concrete process and taken through execution.

I do not replace management or specialist advisers. Management knows the business better than I do, while legal, tax, financial and technical expertise is brought into the project when required.

My job is not to be right. It is to make sure the right questions have been asked before a decision is made.

Working together

Defined first phase. Clear decision. Execution only when it makes sense.

An engagement would normally start with a defined assessment phase: understanding the company, the entrepreneur's objective, the project, the market and the available alternatives.

The objective is to reach a decision that is sufficiently structured to be executed.

If the decision is to proceed, the project moves into an execution phase, with scope and remuneration agreed according to the nature of the assignment.
